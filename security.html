How we back up your data

Your information is protected by several layers of backups, so it can always be recovered.

  • Automatic nightly backupsEvery night your data is backed up for you. There's nothing for you to remember.
  • Undo mistakes, up to 30 days backIf something is deleted or changed by accident, we can roll back to any point in the last 30 days.
  • Separate, secure copiesExtra copies are stored separately: daily copies for 90 days and a monthly copy for a full year.
  • Copies that can't be tampered withBackup copies are protected so they can't be changed or deleted early, by anyone.
  • Tested every monthWe restore a backup every month to prove it works, so you're never left guessing.
  • Instant failure alertsIf a backup ever doesn't run, our team is alerted right away and fixes it.

How we keep your account secure

  • Access by roleOwners, managers and staff each see only what their job needs.
  • Two-step sign-inAn optional second check keeps your account safe even if a password is stolen.
  • Private files, sign-in onlyYour photos, receipts and documents are private and never open to anyone with a link.
  • A full change historySee who changed what and when, for complete peace of mind.
