Software should work for the business

Hi, my name is Austin Stinson from Tallahassee, FL. I am the founder of VersaNext. VNXT was founded because I kept finding that my business, Tech Sourced Solutions, had to operate out of multiple different software tools, which caused many inefficiencies. I kept researching solutions that would let my employees work in one system and run more smoothly. I could not find anything. After building an application customized around my business, it became clear that every small business should have software built specifically for them. VersaNext builds custom software solutions with AI integration that help your business operate more efficiently so you can focus on the things that matter.
