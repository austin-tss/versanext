Our Story

Software should work for the business.

Austin Stinson is a career entrepreneur in Tallahassee. He runs more than one business, and he always has. VersaNext comes from that life: a company should not have to bend itself around someone else's software.

Where the tools got in the way

For the install business at Tech Sourced Solutions, the team was living across multiple software tools. Admin was complicated. The work slowed down. Hours that belonged with customers went to keeping those tools in step with each other.

So they built a homemade app, shaped to the way that business actually runs. One place for the day they already had. The work got smoother, because the software finally fit the business.

The idea that became VersaNext

Once that custom solution was working, Austin saw the bigger truth. The future of software, at every level of business, is not cookie-cutter software a company has to squeeze into. Software should work for the business, and be customized to it. Not the other way around.

That realization became VersaNext. The brand mark is VNXT, and VersaNext is a division of Tech Sourced Solutions. Everyone deserves a custom solution that fills the need in front of them, instead of being confined to one rigid product.

VersaNext is a virtual employee for your business. Custom software, built around how you run, so the everyday work is handled and your people can stay with the customers, the jobs, and the decisions that need them.

Versatile technology solutions to take your organization to the next level.

Here in Tallahassee

This work is local, on purpose. Austin is a Tallahassee person, and VersaNext is focused on local Tallahassee businesses. The aim is a solution you can talk through with someone here, built around the way your company already operates, not a product shipped from somewhere that has never seen your day.

From a career of running more than one business, and from his work with Tech Sourced Solutions, Austin is building VersaNext for companies that want what the install team needed: software that fits.
